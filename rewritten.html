Rewritten: Alien Intervention

A science-fiction movie poster and a theater-display mockup.

Project Overview

Rewritten: Alien Intervention is a conceptual science-fiction movie poster created in Adobe Photoshop. I combined a portrait with futuristic imagery, dramatic lighting, a spacecraft, and promotional typography to suggest a fictional cinematic story. The poster was also placed into a theater-display mockup to show how the finished composition might appear in a public setting. The exercise focused on image masking, motion effects, color, hierarchy, and realistic presentation. By treating the design as both artwork and a promotional object, I explored how cinematic visuals and familiar advertising conventions work together to communicate genre and attract an interested viewing audience more effectively.

Design & Development

I combined photographic elements, a science-fiction setting, and film-style typography, then presented the result inside a theater mockup. This is an unofficial fictional design exercise, not an authorized film advertisement.
