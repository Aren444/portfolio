Responsive web design

FSBO Real Estate

A multipage real estate site with property details, photography, and financing information.

Project Overview

This FSBO Real Estate project presents a residential property through a responsive multipage website. I organized the experience around the information prospective buyers or renters would need: property photographs, details, financing information, and a way to make contact. The layout was designed to keep navigation predictable while giving the photographs enough space to communicate the property's features. Working on a real estate subject challenged me to prioritize clarity and usefulness over decoration. The completed website demonstrates my ability to build for a different audience and industry, using straightforward page structures to make information easier to locate and understand on devices.

Design & Development

I organized property photographs and information into separate pages, including a gallery, financing information, and contact details. My focus was on a clear browsing path and layout that adapts to different screen sizes.
