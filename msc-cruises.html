Usability research

MSC Cruises

A usability review of cruise booking navigation, functionality, and mobile experience.

Project Overview

For this usability review, I examined the MSC Cruises booking experience to identify obstacles that could interrupt trip planning or purchase decisions. My evaluation considered navigation, information clarity, form behavior, and mobile responsiveness. I compared aspects of the experience with a competing cruise provider and incorporated feedback from a customer interview. The presentation identifies usability issues, assigns severity, and proposes improvements such as clearer cabin selection, better explanations of fares, and retaining entered information. This project communicates my ability to assess an existing digital product, connect findings to recognized usability principles, and recommend changes that better support real cruise customers.

Design & Development

I assessed booking flows, compared a competing service, and organized issues by navigation, content, functionality, and responsiveness. I turned these observations into specific recommendations tied to usability principles.

CMST 290 – Interactive Design · 2024 · Usability analysis, presentation · Independent project, all work by Aren Bird
