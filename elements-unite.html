Elements Unite

A Photoshop composition using natural elements and a fragmented portrait.

Project Overview

Elements Unite is a digital composition created in Adobe Photoshop. The artwork fragments a portrait into curved sections, introducing imagery associated with fire, water, sky, and forest. I used layered images, masking, and contrasting color to explore the relationship between people and the natural world. Dark negative space isolates the portrait while drawing attention to the eyes and the different textures inside the composition. The project gave me an opportunity to experiment with nontraditional portrait construction instead of straightforward retouching. It demonstrates my ability to combine photographic sources into an expressive image with a recognizable visual theme and strong contrast.

Design & Development

I assembled the composition using layers and masks, selecting natural imagery and contrasting colors to shape the fragmented portrait. The finished image emphasizes composition rather than realistic portrait restoration.
