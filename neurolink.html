NeuroLink

A fictional brain-interface advertising campaign using AI-assisted media production.

Radio Advertisement

Project Overview

NeuroLink is a fictional brain-interface product created for a multimedia advertising assignment. I developed a futuristic product concept and adapted its message for different channels, including social media, a blog, a billboard, print advertising, and audio. The campaign imagines communication, photography, and information access controlled through thought. Its marketing materials target adults interested in technological convenience, while the presentation explores the product's features and positioning. AI-assisted creative tools contributed to the campaign, including a generated promotional rap. Together, these deliverables demonstrate the ability to maintain a consistent idea while changing format, tone, and presentation for different media and intended audiences.

Design & Development

I established the fictional product positioning, drafted promotional copy, and built advertising examples for several media. The rap audio was AI-generated as part of the campaign. NeuroLink is a speculative product, not an available medical device.
